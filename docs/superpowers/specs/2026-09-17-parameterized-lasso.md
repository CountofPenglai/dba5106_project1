# Parameterized LASSO portfolio design

Approved in conversation: keep one understandable linear LASSO model with constraints and a parameterized growth objective. Implement with multiple agents; do not push this work without a new request.

The model remains `w = EW - N @ beta`, trained daily on the preceding 252 observations with fixed LASSO strength `1e-5`. Objective: training variance + L1 penalty - eta * mean(log1p(Rw)) + gamma * EW training variance * normalized exposure penalty. The exposure penalty is `max(0, (gross - 1)/(gross_limit - 1))**2`; with the default gross limit 1.5 it is zero at gross 1 and one at gross 1.5. At gross_limit=1 define the exposure penalty as zero because the hard limit already prohibits short exposure. eta and gamma are nonnegative. Defaults remain eta=.01 and gamma=0, so old results remain reproducible. Keep gross<=1.5, training volatility<=EW volatility, and per-scenario wealth>=1e-6. Solvers and feasibility verification remain unchanged.

Train beta using historical returns only. Select eta and gamma with bounded validation search, never jointly optimize them in the training loss. Search early stopping is not solver-iteration early stopping. Default coarse grid: eta in (0,.003,.01), gamma in (0,.1,1). Evaluate complete batches on identical dates. Reward bound .03; exposure strength bound 3. Neighbors halve/double one positive coordinate; from zero move to half the smallest positive coarse value. Deduplicate candidates. Never start a batch that would exceed the budget.

Search defaults: maximum 15 candidates, patience 1 complete refinement batch, cumulative-return min improvement .002 (0.2 percentage points). Coarse initialization always completes. Refine around the raw cumulative-return best candidate; monitor the greatest observed cumulative return versus the last significant-improvement anchor. Restore a recorded selection on stop. Among candidates within .002 of the maximum, prefer lower eta, then lower actual average gross exposure, then lower gamma as a final deterministic tie break. Record both the raw-best and selected scores. Numerical failures are recorded and excluded; no shortened backtests or fake returns.

Experiment: screen all nine coarse candidates on 2025-07-01..2025-12-31 using 252-day rolling fits. Validation 2026-01-02..2026-06-30 starts with (0,0), (.01,0), historical selection and its zero-reward/zero-exposure ablations (deduplicated). Permit at most six validation evaluations beyond initialization, so the union of searched parameter pairs has at most 15 members. A candidate repeated across periods counts as one distinct configuration but two separate evaluations, both reported. Historical scores do not enter the validation patience monitor or final ranking. No future returns enter either phase's fits, scaling or selection.

Freeze the final choice before comparing EW, original fixed LASSO, constraints-only, fixed reward, selected reward-only, selected exposure-only and selected joint objective. Final two ablations are descriptive evaluations, never fed back into selection. Label 2026 validation results as development results, not independent testing. Final August test is unavailable in the local data ending 2026-07-31. No transaction costs are modeled.

Export search records, per-candidate full returns/weights, selected parameters, stopping reasons, dates, full settings, hashes and package versions. Reject nonempty output directories. Validate chronological nonoverlapping phases and complete data coverage. Keep the existing run_example.py defaults and all existing strategies intact. Add a separate run_tuning.py entry point; a small selection module is preferable to a general optimization framework.
